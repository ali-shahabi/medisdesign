## 11. Prototype rules

- `<html lang="fa" dir="rtl">`, UTF-8, one standalone file per design, inline CSS + JS.
- Every visible number in Persian digits (۰۱۲۳۴۵۶۷۸۹).
- Navigation, hero, services, projects, stats, partners, magazine, contact, footer.
  Products may be folded into services where the direction is not product-led.
- All internal links are anchors on the same page; external links may be `#`.
- No lorem ipsum, no English filler sentences; English only as deliberate micro-labels
  (e.g. `MEDIS / ACOUSTIC ARCHITECTURE`, plate numbers).
- Responsive at 1440, 768 and 390; no horizontal overflow at 390.
- Accessibility: skip link, focus-visible styles, alt text, `prefers-reduced-motion`.
- Images must have width/height or aspect-ratio to avoid layout shift; lazy-load below fold.
